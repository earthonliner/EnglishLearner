🗣️ Be the trend spotter

I used to think   was a fad / trend.

Now I think it is a  . First, it can / can't make money because  .

Second, it links / doesn't link with  .

Third, it appeals to  .

So, like planking / e-commerce, it will probably  .

Use the author's signal words — First, Second, Third — to organise your reasons, just like paragraph 3 does.
