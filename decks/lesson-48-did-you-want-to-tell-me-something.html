Grammar 2 · 过去完成时

Which happened first?

Rule: when two things are both in the past, the earlier one takes had + done. "My dentist had just pulled out a tooth and had told me to rest" — both before "I tried to say something".
Tap the events in time order. Then say: which ones use had done?
