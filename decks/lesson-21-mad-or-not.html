Lead-in · How loud?

Grammar · The passive machine

Who did it? Nobody knows – or nobody cares

modal + be + V3Planes can be heard night and day. · It could not be used. · This house will be knocked down.
modal + have been + V3A hundred people must have been driven away. (a guess about the past)
have/has been + V3I have been offered a large sum of money. (present perfect passive)
✈️

Why passive here? The man doesn't know (or doesn't care) WHO built the airport or WHO offered the money – the action matters, not the agent.

Wrap-up

So – mad, or not?

💬 Debate (3 min)

  • "Everybody says I must be mad and they are probably right." Is he mad, brave, or just stubborn? Vote again – did anyone change sides?
  • If YOU were the airport, what would you offer him instead of money?
  • What is one thing you would never sell, at any price?

📝 Homework

  • Rewrite the story as a 60–80 word news report. Use at least 4 passives: can be heard, was built, has been offered, will be knocked down.
  • Sentence Builder Level 4 (Combine + Fix).
  • Three true sentences: … is slowly driving me mad.

Exit ticket: one sentence each, in the passive, about your own home or school.
