Wrap-up

Three things to take away

📌 Remember

  • Finished time → past simple. In 2011, ago, yesterday never go with have done.
  • Since / for / already / yet / ever → present perfect. It connects then to now.
  • Evidence or a decision already made → going to. Deciding right now, a promise or an opinion → will.
Exit ticket: tell me one thing you're going to do this week, and one thing you think will happen in 2030.
