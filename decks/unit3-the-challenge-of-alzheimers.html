AGING SOCIETY · SIMPLE DEFINITION

A society in which older people make up a growing share of the population.

Think: fewer children + longer lives = a new population shape.
Think first · No single answer

Is an aging society good or bad?

Choose a side first. Then click both cards and see whether your view becomes more balanced.

🌱

Opportunities

  • People live longer, healthier lives.
  • Older adults have skills and experience.
Think about: volunteering, “silver economy” products, grandparents supporting families.
⚖️

Challenges

  • Healthcare and pensions cost more.
  • Fewer workers support more retirees.
Think about: hospitals, caregivers, taxes, and pressure on working families.
Talk: Which matters more—living longer or the cost of care? Can a society have both opportunities and challenges?
Real-world snapshot · 2024 data

Japan and China: two aging societies

🇯🇵

Japan

29.3%of the population is aged 65 or above
  • One of the world's oldest populations
  • More empty homes in some rural areas
  • Robots and technology help with elder care
  • Many older adults continue working
SOURCE · Statistics Bureau of Japan, 2024 estimate
🇨🇳

China

15.6%of the population is aged 65 or above
  • About 220 million people are 65+
  • Smaller families may care for several older relatives
  • Community meal services and activity centers are growing
  • Demand for nurses and caregivers is rising
SOURCE · National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2024

Language lab · Notice and use

Expressions worth taking away

Click a phrase → reveal its use

for the first time in history
Use it for an unprecedented change.
For the first time in history, …
历史上第一次
have a higher chance of doing
Use it to describe greater possibility or risk.
Older adults have a higher chance of …
更有可能做……
put pressure on
A useful cause-and-effect collocation.
Rising costs put pressure on …
给……带来压力
become unable to do
A gradual loss of ability.
Some patients may become unable to …
变得无法做……
require constant care
“Require” is more formal than “need”.
The patient requires constant care.
需要持续照护
